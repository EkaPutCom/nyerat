# Chapter 1: The Harbor

Raka was seventeen years old when he first saw the ship White Seagull moored at the East Harbor. The hull of the ship was painted a clean white, and its sails were sea blue.

## The Meeting

On the pier, Raka met Laras, the daughter of a spice merchant. Laras carried a letter from her father and would not show it to anyone.

Captain Hasan welcomed them at the gangway. He was an old man with a deep voice, and had led voyages to the east for thirty years.

## The Departure

They left at dawn. Raka stood at the bow and counted the seagulls that followed the ship until they vanished in the fog.
